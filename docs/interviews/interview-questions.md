# Interview questions (same for all respondents)


1. Could you tell us how you learn C# or prepare for WorldSkills (or how you teach it)? What do you do during a typical week?
2. How do you notice that a student, or you yourself, is struggling? At what point does it usually become visible?
3. Has it happened that it was unclear which assessment criteria cost you points? How do you deal with that?
4. Imagine a platform that shows the teacher a list of students who may need help, with the reasons. Would that be useful? What should such a list include?
5. The platform will record activity on the site: which pages you open, how long you spend, and your attempts at exercises. How do you feel about that, and under what conditions would you agree?
